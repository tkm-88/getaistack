## What to Look for When Choosing an AI Builder

1. **Local Payment Gateway Integration:** If you are running an online shop, ensure the platform easily supports Indian payment gateways like Razorpay, Cashfree, or PhonePe PG.
2. **Export Capabilities & Lock-in:** Ensure the platform lets you connect custom domains like `.in` without charging exorbitant premium tier fees.
3. **Core Web Vitals:** A fast-loading site directly impacts your Google SEO ranking in regional search results.
