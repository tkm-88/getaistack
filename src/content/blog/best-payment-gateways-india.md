Accepting payments online in India requires balancing three critical factors: **high transaction success rates on UPI**, **smooth digital onboarding (KYC)**, and **support for international currency transactions**.

With regulatory updates from the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) regarding Payment Aggregator (PA) licensing, selecting a reliable, fully compliant payment partner is vital to prevent sudden checkout disruptions or frozen settlements.

In this guide, we compare the **top 3 payment gateways for Indian businesses and creators**, evaluating their transaction fee structure, developer API documentation, UPI Intent support, and international payment features.

## 1. Razorpay (Best Overall for Tech Startups, Subscriptions & SaaS)

**Razorpay** is the market leader for modern tech businesses in India, offering the most mature developer ecosystem, checkout UI, and automation capabilities.

### Key Highlights:
* **Razorpay Subscriptions & UPI AutoPay:** Best-in-class support for recurring subscription billing on both Indian debit/credit cards and UPI AutoPay apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm).
* **Developer Ecosystem:** Extensive SDKs for JavaScript, Python, Node, React Native, Flutter, and pre-built integrations for WooCommerce, Shopify, and Astro.
* **International Currency Support:** Enables international payments with dynamic currency conversion (DCC) across 100+ global currencies.

---

## 2. Cashfree Payments (Best for Bulk Payouts & Marketplaces)

**Cashfree** has built a solid reputation for handling high-volume transaction loads, vendor payouts, and automated split settlements.

### Key Highlights:
* **Cashfree Payouts API:** Instant automated 24/7 bank disbursements to vendors, affiliates, or gig workers via IMPS, UPI, and cards.
* **Flow Checkout:** Highly customizable checkout flows with headless SDK options for custom app designs.
* **Verification Suite:** Built-in APIs to verify customer bank accounts, PAN cards, and Aadhaar during onboarding.

---

## 3. PhonePe Payment Gateway (Best for Low Transaction Costs on Domestic UPI)

Backed by PhonePe's massive consumer UPI user base, **PhonePe PG** is an attractive option for local eCommerce stores and direct-to-consumer (D2C) brands looking for cost savings.

### Key Highlights:
* **High UPI Success Rates:** Leverages direct integration with the PhonePe consumer ecosystem for rapid one-click UPI checkout.
* **Transparent Pricing:** Competitive pricing models tailored for domestic Indian transactions.
* **Easy Plugin Setup:** Quick-start plugins available for standard eCommerce platforms like WooCommerce and Shopify.

---

## Key Factors to Consider Before Choosing

1. **International Card Compliance:** If you sell digital products, courses, or SaaS to customers outside India, Razorpay provides the smoothest onboarding for international payment rails.
2. **Settlement Cycles:** Standard settlement is usually **T+2 or T+3 business days**. Look into instant settlement add-ons if cash flow is critical.
3. **UPI Intent Flow:** Ensure your checkout supports native UPI Intent (opening the payment app directly on mobile) rather than requiring users to manually type their VPA/UPI ID.

---

## Final Recommendation

* If you are launching a **SaaS product, digital software stack, or international business**, choose **Razorpay**.
* If you run a **marketplace requiring complex automated payouts to vendors**, choose **Cashfree**.
* If you run a **purely domestic Indian eCommerce brand focused on UPI**, look into **PhonePe Payment Gateway**.
